# Hero photos

Drop real photos here (the site uses them automatically; the `*-art.svg` drawings show until then):

- `basketball.jpg`   — background of the Basketball page hero
- `bodybuilding.jpg` — background of the Bodybuilding page hero

Recommended: landscape, at least 2400×1400 px, under ~400 KB (JPEG quality ~70), subject away from the centre-left where the title sits.
Use photos you own or with a free commercial licence (e.g. Unsplash / Pexels) and keep the licence info.
